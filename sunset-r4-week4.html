Sunset R4’s League Night Rules

The attached FTLO Outdoor Modified Grass Rules cover gameplay. These additional rules explain how Sunset R4’s league nights will operate.
  1. 01
    Check in
    • Check in with the League Coordinators when you arrive.
    • Coordinators will confirm your pool and starting court.
    • Teams may move into different pools in future weeks based on their results.
  2. 02
    Night format
    • Optional warm-up begins at 6:00 PM.
    • First serve begins at 6:10 PM.
    • Teams will normally be placed in pools of 4 teams.
    • Each team will play 3 matches per night.
    • Matches begin and end on the League Coordinator's whistle.
  3. 03
    Match scoring
    For regular 4-team pools:
    • Matches are best 2 out of 3 sets.
    • Sets are played to 21, 21 and 5, time permitting.
    • All sets are capped. Teams do not need to win by two.
    • A set ends when a team reaches the score cap or when the coordinator whistles to end the match.
    • If the score is tied when time expires, play one deciding point.
    • The final serve of the night must begin before the final whistle.
  4. 04
    Reporting scores
    • At the end of every match, report the score of each set to a League Coordinator.
    • Coordinators will record the results on the league board.
    • Results will help determine future pool placement.
    • Teams should report promptly so the next round can begin on time.
  5. 05
    Late teams
    • The match clock begins on the League Coordinator's opening whistle.
    • Teams receive a 10-minute grace period.
    • After 10 minutes, the present team receives a 21–0 set win.
    • If the late team arrives afterward, the remaining sets may still be played within the original match time.
    • If the team does not arrive, the official result is 21–0, 21–0.
  6. 06
    Ghost rule
    • Teams may still play with only 2 players.
    • However, the official recorded result will be a 21–0, 21–0 loss for the team with only 2 players.
    • The match may still be played, but the on-court score will not replace the official result.
  7. 07
    Participation waiver
    • All players and substitutes must complete the FTLO Outdoor Participation Waiver before playing.
    • The waiver only needs to be completed once during the summer.
    • Captains are responsible for making sure all players and substitutes have completed it.
    Waiver: www.ftlovolleyball.ca/outdoor-tourney/
  8. 08
    Rainouts & completed nights
    Rainouts and completed-night decisions follow the FTLO Outdoor Weather Policy:
    www.ftlovolleyball.ca/policies/

FTLO Volleyball

Outdoor Modified Grass Rules
FTLO
  1. 1.
    Rally Point scoring. Normally win by 2, sometimes capped.
  2. 2.
    Serves that touch the net and over are considered live.
    • •Players cannot touch the net at all.
  3. 3.
    Rotation of servers on each side-out is mandatory, not rotation of positions.
  4. 4.
    Receptions of serves, attacks, or balls over the net can be taken in a volley (open hand pass). Lifts are not permitted.
  5. 5.
    Attacks may be a hit, roll-shot, closed-hand poke. Open hand tips not allowed.
    • •NOT allowed to attack the ball if it’s entirely in the opponent’s space.
  6. 6.
    Volleys made over the net must be done with shoulders and feet squared to the setting target, either forwards or backwards.
    • •“Double-Hit” setting faults can only be called by your own team or referee.
  7. 7.
    Blocks do not count as a touch.
    • •Are illegal if it clearly interferes with an opponent’s 3rd contact attempt.
    • •i.e. Reaching over the net and blocking the ball before their 3rd contact is made.
  8. 8.
    Male Specific Rules
    • •Back row jump attacks permitted if last point of foot contact was outside the 3m line.
    • •May only block males, unless specific females allow it for each set.
    • •“2-line rule” — balls played over the net that do not cross the two lines (personal 3m and center line, OR center line and opponent’s 3m) result in: point to opponent if left untouched, or play on if opponents touch the ball.
    • •Soft blocks on females count as the team’s first contact, or are a fault if the male’s block goes back to the female’s side.
  9. 9.
    Skyball encouraged when at set-point and with a large lead.
    • •House rules for skyball re-serves. IYKYK.
  10. 10.
    When In Doubt, Re-serve!
